# wilkosz.com.au - notes for agents

- Text-only static site. `index.html` is generated by `build.py` from `data/*.json`; never edit it by hand.
- Agent-maintained data goes through `merge.py` with one run JSON (see `.claude/skills/market-research/SKILL.md`).
- Owner-maintained: `data/profile.json`, `data/holdings.json`, `data/loves.json`. Do not edit unless asked.
- Never render units, prices, dollar values or cost basis for the owner's holdings - weights and takes only.
- The owner reads the site on a phone, last thing at night and first thing in the morning (AEST).
  Everything published must be scannable on a narrow screen: lead with what changed, short bullets,
  no wide tables, numbers over adjectives.
- Keep the tone plain. No hype, no emoji.
