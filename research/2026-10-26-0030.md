## Market summary

A second losing week for growth: in the week to Fri Oct 23 the S&P 500 fell 0.5% to 7,623.68 and the Nasdaq 0.7% to 25,955.34, while the Dow (+0.4%) and Russell 2000 (+2.0%) rose. Energy-for-AI stayed strong: Bloom Energy grew Q3 revenue 138% and raised guidance for the third time this year. Brazil's Oct 25 runoff was still undecided at publication, with Lula narrowly ahead at about two-thirds counted.
