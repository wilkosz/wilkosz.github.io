# Research run 2026-08-24T22:24 UTC

## Market summary

As of late August 2026, global equities sit near all-time highs on the back of an AI capex supercycle (consensus hyperscaler capex ~$733B for 2026 and ~$939B for 2027; the big four spent $166B in the June quarter alone, +87% YoY), but July delivered a sharp reality check: chip stocks shed over $1 trillion as investors questioned capex returns, with the SOX falling roughly 30% from its June peak before partially recovering. Macro is less friendly than a year ago: the Fed held in July on a 9-3 split with dissents favoring a hike, an energy shock tied to Strait of Hormuz disruptions has oil near $80 with upside risk, futures price ~65% odds of a 25bp hike in September, and tariffs remain a cost headwind for industrials (GE Vernova flagged a $100-200M hit). Power is the binding constraint on AI buildout, so gas turbines, fuel cells, nuclear PPAs and grid/storage are all seeing record backlogs, while Chinese battery and storage leaders are posting 40-90% growth despite a domestic EV price war. Nvidia reports Aug 26 and Broadcom Sept 2, the next major tests of AI sentiment; the July selloff has opened entry points in high-quality AI infrastructure names, whereas nuclear SMR pure-plays have de-rated 30-40% YTD on commercialization timelines and dilution.
